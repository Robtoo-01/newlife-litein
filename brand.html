Brand guide · for staff & volunteers

Brand & component library

Everything needed to keep New Life looking like New Life — on this website, on posters, on social media and in print.

01 · Logo

The mark: a cross over a new dawn.

The cross from our original logo, refined, standing over a rising horizon — new life in Christ, every morning. It's built on a 64-unit grid so it stays crisp at any size.

Construction

  • Grid: 64 × 64 units, tile corner radius 15.
  • Cross: vertical 32,10 → 32,50; bar 21 → 43 at y 23; stroke 5.6, round caps.
  • Horizon: arc from 10,46 to 54,46 peaking at y 36.5; stroke 3.2, 55% opacity.
  • Wordmark: “New Life” Fraunces SemiBold, −1.3% tracking. “CHURCH · LITEIN” Instrument Sans SemiBold, caps, +25% tracking, one-third the cap height.

Clear space & size

  • Keep clear space on every side equal to half the tile width (32 units).
  • Minimum size: icon 16 px (favicon) / 8 mm in print; full lockup 120 px wide / 30 mm.
  • Below 120 px, use the icon alone.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or skew the logo.
  • Change the colours outside the six approved versions.
  • Put the primary logo on busy photos — use reversed on a navy panel.
  • Add shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font.

Production note: the SVG wordmarks use live text. Before sending to a printer or sign-maker, open them in Figma/Illustrator/Inkscape with Fraunces and Instrument Sans installed (both free from Google Fonts) and convert text to outlines.

05 · Voice & photography

Warm, plain, hopeful.

  • Write as if inviting a neighbour: short sentences, “you” and “we”, no insider words without explanation.
  • Every section ends in one clear next step: Plan your visit, Watch, Request prayer, Join a group, Give.
  • Use real photos of our church family, with permission. Natural light, real moments — no stock photos.
  • Kiswahili is welcome: “Karibu” sets the tone.

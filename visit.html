What to expect

A Sunday morning at New Life.

  1. A warm welcome

    Ushers will greet you at the door and help you find a seat. Arriving a little before 9:00 AM gives you time to settle in.

  2. Praise & worship

    Joyful singing led by our choirs and worship team. Join in, or simply take it in.

  3. The Word

    A clear, practical Bible message you can carry into your week.

  4. Prayer

    Prayer for the needs in the room — healing, families, breakthrough. Take part as much or as little as you're comfortable with.

  5. Stay & connect

    Ask an usher if you'd like to speak with Pastor Rop or the prayer team after the service.

Finding us

Opposite Koiwa Road junction, near Litein Shopping Centre

Children

Bring the whole family.

Every Sunday from 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM, while adults are in the main service, children meet in their own Sunday school classes — worshipping, learning God's Word and making friends.

  • Ages: Nursery (0–3), Beginners (4–7), Primary (8–12) and Teens Church (13–17).
  • First visit: arrive a few minutes before 10:30 AM and a Sunday school teacher will help you register your child.
  • Safe pick-up: a parent or guardian signs children in, and at 12:30 PM children are released only to that adult or someone you've named.
Programme & child safety

Good to know

  • What to wear: come as you are. Some wear their Sunday best, others everyday clothes — both are welcome.
  • Language: English and Kiswahili, with interpretation into the local language.
  • Giving: voluntary, and never a condition for prayer or ministry. Guests are not expected to give.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What time are services?

Sunday worship runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, and is streamed live on YouTube from 9:30 AM. Special programmes, such as anointing weeks, usually run from 1:00 PM. There's an online prayer service on Facebook every evening from 8:00 PM.

Where exactly is the church?

Opposite Koiwa Road junction, near Litein Shopping Centre in Litein, Kericho County. Any boda boda rider in town can bring you. Get directions.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some people wear their Sunday best, others come in everyday clothes — both are welcome.

Is there something for children?

Yes! Sunday school runs every Sunday from 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM, in age groups from nursery to teens. A teacher will help you register your child on your first visit. See the programme and how we keep children safe.

Can I get personal prayer or counselling?

Yes. Use the prayer form, speak to an usher after the service, or call/WhatsApp 0741 277 938 to arrange time with Pastor Rop. The church office is open 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.

Do I have to give?

No. Giving is voluntary and is never a condition for prayer, counselling or ministry.

Can I watch if I can't come in person?

Yes — sermons are on YouTube (@pastorrop), and Pastor Rop goes live on Facebook for the nightly prayer service.

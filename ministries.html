Children

Kids & Sunday school

Children are a joyful and important part of our church family. Every Sunday from 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM, while the adults are in the main service, children meet in their own Sunday school classes. They worship, learn God's Word and make friends.

Age groups

  • Nursery (0–3 years): a safe, quiet room with toys and simple songs. Parents or carers are welcome to stay with very young children.
  • Beginners (4–7 years): Bible stories, songs, memory verses and colouring or crafts.
  • Primary (8–12 years): Bible lessons, memory verses, discussion, quizzes and activities.
  • Teens (13–17 years): Teens Church, with praise and worship, teaching on faith and life issues, and mentorship.

Sunday programme

  • 10:30 AM — children are signed in, followed by an opening prayer
  • 10:45 AM — praise and worship (English and Kiswahili songs)
  • 11:15 AM — Bible lesson in age groups
  • 11:50 AM — memory verse, activity or craft
  • 12:15 PM — offering, closing prayer and getting ready for pick-up
  • 12:30 PM — children are signed out and collected by parents

Check-in & pick-up

  • On your first Sunday, a Sunday school teacher will help you register your child. We ask for the child's name and age, the parent's name and phone number, and any allergies or medical needs.
  • Each Sunday, a parent or guardian signs the child in at the Sunday school desk before class.
  • At 12:30 PM, children are released only to the parent or guardian who signed them in, or to someone the parent has named beforehand.
  • Children wait in class until they are collected, and are not allowed to leave the church compound on their own.
  • If your child needs you during class, a teacher will call you from the main service.

Keeping children safe

  • All Sunday school teachers are committed church members, approved by church leadership and trained in our child protection policy.
  • At least two teachers are always present with each class. No adult is ever alone with a child out of sight.
  • Children stay in the Sunday school area, with a teacher escorting them to the washrooms.
  • Teachers are trained to recognise and report any concern. Every concern is taken seriously and handled confidentially, in line with the Children Act 2022.
  • Photos of children are only taken or shared with a parent's permission.

Parents can raise any concern with our Child Protection Officer. Childline Kenya: call 116 (free, 24 hours) for any child in need of help or protection.

Visiting with children? Arrive a few minutes before 10:30 AM and our Sunday school team will welcome you and help your child settle in. Children of all ages are welcome!

  • Sundays, 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM
  • Ages 0–17, in four groups

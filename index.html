Your first Sunday, made simple.

No need to know the songs or what to wear. Here's what a Sunday morning looks like.

  1. Arrive by 9:00 AM

    We're opposite Koiwa Road junction, near Litein Shopping Centre. Parking is close by, and any boda boda rider knows the way.

  2. Worship & the Word

    Joyful praise, choir ministry and clear Bible teaching, in English and Kiswahili.

  3. Stay for prayer

    Every service makes room for prayer. Ushers can connect you with Pastor Rop or the prayer team.

Who we are

Raising disciples, transforming lives, impacting our world.

We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church. Every Sunday our sanctuary near Litein Shopping Centre fills with families, young people, choirs and fellowships — and there's always room for one more.

Prayer first

We bring every need to God, together — on Sundays, every night online, and one to one.

Rooted in the Word

Clear Bible teaching you can carry into Monday morning.

Family for everyone

Fellowships, choirs and care that last well beyond Sunday.

Our story & beliefs

Ministries

Ways to meet with God — and each other.

Sunday worship

Praise, a practical Bible message and prayer for your needs. The best place to start.

Holy Communion

Monthly seasons at the Lord's Table to remember the cross and receive restoration.

Prayer & counselling

Talk and pray with Pastor Rop or the prayer team — in person, by phone or online.

Healing & deliverance

Choirs & fellowships

Kids & youth

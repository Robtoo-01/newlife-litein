FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure about something? Call or WhatsApp us — we're glad to help.

Call 0741 277 938
What time are services?
Sunday worship runs from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM. Special programmes such as anointing weeks usually run from 1:00 PM, and there's an online prayer service on Facebook every evening from 8:00 PM.
Where exactly is the church?
Opposite Koiwa Road junction, near Litein Shopping Centre in Litein, Kericho County. Any boda boda rider in town can bring you to New Life Church. Get directions.
What should I wear?
Come as you are. Some people wear their Sunday best, others come in everyday clothes — both are welcome.
Is there something for children?
Children are welcome in the service with their families. Ask an usher about Sunday school when you arrive.
Can I get personal prayer or counselling?
Yes. Use the prayer form, speak to an usher after the service, or call/WhatsApp 0741 277 938 to arrange time with Pastor Rop. The church office is open 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
Do I have to give?
No. Giving is voluntary and is never a condition for prayer, counselling or ministry.
Can I watch if I can't come in person?
Yes — sermons are on YouTube (@pastorrop) and Pastor Rop goes live on Facebook for the nightly prayer service.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

New Life Prayer Center and Church – Litein (“we”) respects your privacy. This page explains what happens to information you share through this website.

What we collect

  • This website does not store form submissions. When you use the prayer form, your message opens in WhatsApp and is sent only when you press send.
  • Messages sent to 0741 277 938 may include your name, phone number and the content you choose to share.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, pray for you or arrange counselling.
  • Prayer requests are seen only by Pastor Rop and the prayer team and are never published.
  • Testimonies are shared publicly only with your permission, using first name and town.

Third-party services

Embedded YouTube videos may set cookies when you press play. Their own privacy policies apply. Videos load only when you press play.

Your choices

You may ask us to delete your messages or remove a photo or testimony at any time by calling or messaging 0741 277 938.

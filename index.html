About us

Raising disciples, transforming lives, impacting our world

New Life Prayer Center and Church grew from a simple conviction: God still meets people in prayer, heals the broken and sets captives free. Today our sanctuary near Litein Shopping Centre fills every Sunday with families, young people, choirs and fellowships.

We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church. Services are in English and Kiswahili, and everyone — searching, hurting or simply new in town — is welcome.

  • Prayer firstWe bring every need to God, together.
  • Rooted in the WordClear Bible teaching for everyday life.
  • Family for everyoneFellowships, choirs and care that last beyond Sunday.
What to expect on Sunday

Pastor Rop

Senior Pastor, New Life Church Litein

Pastor Rop leads the church with a passion for prayer and the transforming power of the Gospel. He leads Sunday worship, Holy Communion and testimony services, outreach crusades such as Kibugat, and the nightly online prayer service.

His teaching calls believers to stand firm, to see setbacks as God's redirection, and to trust God for healing, deliverance and breakthrough.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure about something? Call or WhatsApp us — we're glad to help.

Call 0741 277 938
What time are services?
Sunday worship runs from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM. Special programmes such as anointing weeks usually run from 1:00 PM, and there's an online prayer service on Facebook every evening from 8:00 PM.
Where exactly is the church?
Opposite Koiwa Road junction, near Litein Shopping Centre in Litein, Kericho County. Any boda boda rider in town can bring you to New Life Church. See the map.
What should I wear?
Come as you are. Some people wear their Sunday best, others come in everyday clothes — both are welcome.
Is there something for children?
Children are welcome in the service with their families. Ask an usher about Sunday school when you arrive.
Can I get personal prayer or counselling?
Yes. Use the form below, speak to an usher after the service, or call/WhatsApp 0741 277 938 to arrange time with Pastor Rop. The church office is open 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
Do I have to give?
No. Giving is voluntary and is never a condition for prayer, counselling or ministry.
Can I watch if I can't come in person?
Yes — sermons are on YouTube (@pastorrop) and Pastor Rop goes live on Facebook for the nightly prayer service.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

How we use it
